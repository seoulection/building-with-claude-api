# Nutrition Plan for Tennis Performance

## Pre-Match (2-3 hours before)

**Balanced meal:**
- Whole grain toast or rice with hummus
- Banana or berries
- Greek yogurt or cottage cheese
- Water (500ml)

**30 mins before court:**
- Sports drink (6-8% carbs) or diluted juice
- Easily digestible carbs (dates, energy bar, or applesauce)

## During Play

**Every 60-90 minutes:**
- Sports drink with electrolytes (sodium, potassium)
- Water between sets
- Quick carbs: banana, energy gel, or dried fruit

## Between Matches

**Immediately after (0-30 mins):**
- Chocolate milk or smoothie (carbs + protein)
- Fruit + Greek yogurt
- Electrolyte drink

**30-90 mins between matches:**
- Pasta with tomato sauce and cheese
- Sandwich with hummus and vegetables
- Rice cakes with jam
- Hydrate consistently

## Daily Hydration
- **Base:** 3-4 liters water daily
- **Add:** Electrolyte drinks on match days
- **Monitor:** Urine color (pale yellow = well-hydrated)

## Avoid During Play
- High fiber foods
- Fatty/greasy items
- Anything untested

**Note:** Adjust portions based on match intensity and recovery needs. Consider consulting a sports dietitian for personalized macro targets.
